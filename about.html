About ChoteGames

ChoteGames is a collection of simple browser games made to be easy to start and fun to replay.

Some games are built for very short sessions, while others can keep you playing for longer. The common idea is simple: open a game, understand it quickly, play instantly and try to improve.

Our current games include Tap Challenge, XO Slide and Bakru Dash. We plan to add more original games over time.

Many games also include personal bests or challenge features so you can try to beat your own score or challenge a friend.

No account is required to start playing.
