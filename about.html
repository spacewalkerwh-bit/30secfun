About 30secfun

30secfun is a simple collection of quick browser games made for those small moments when you have a minute to spare.

Our idea is simple: play fast, beat your best, and challenge your friends.

Our first game, Tap Challenge, gives you 30 seconds to tap as fast as you can. No account or sign-up is required.

We are building 30secfun around short, easy-to-start games that are fun to replay and easy to share.
