Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 30secfun handles information when you use our website and games.

Information you provide

30secfun does not require an account or sign-up. If you choose to enter a player name, that name may be included in a challenge link or shared challenge message. Please avoid entering sensitive or private information as your player name.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and game engagement, such as visits and interactions. Analytics services may collect information about your browser, device and usage of the website through cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies and similar technologies

30secfun and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and, if advertising is introduced in the future, for advertising-related purposes. If our use of these technologies changes materially, this policy may be updated.

Sharing of information

We may use third-party service providers, such as analytics providers, to help operate and understand the website. We do not ask users to submit sensitive personal information through the game.

Children

30secfun is a general-audience casual gaming website. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children.

External services

Challenge sharing may use features provided by your browser or messaging/social applications. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, analytics, advertising, or other services change. The “Last updated” date above will indicate the latest revision.
