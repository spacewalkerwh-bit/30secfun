Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ChoteGames handles information when you use our website and games.

ChoteGames does not require an account or sign-up. Some games may let you enter an optional player name or create a challenge link. Please do not enter sensitive personal information into game fields.

We use Google Analytics to understand website traffic and game engagement. Analytics services may collect information about your browser, device and use of the website through cookies or similar technologies.

Cookies and similar technologies

ChoteGames and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies for analytics and, if advertising is introduced in the future, for advertising-related purposes.

Sharing

We may use third-party service providers, such as analytics providers, to help operate and understand the website. Challenge sharing may also use browser or messaging features provided by third parties.

Advertising

If advertising is introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant advertising services and their use of cookies or similar technologies.

Contact

Changes
